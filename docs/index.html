anxiety

noun

very frequent, 425.7 per million

A strong feeling of worry or nervousness about something that is happening or might happen. In the comments it is mostly spoken of as a named condition that people have, are diagnosed with, or try to reduce.

From the comments

  • Every time I experience anxiety or panic attack I come back to this video.Dry Creek Wrangler School
  • I went to a therapist and she told me I had light depression and moderate to high anxiety.Kati Morton
  • I've been dealing with serious anxiety for a while.Frederik Imbo (TEDx)
  • Social anxiety, depression are my diagnosis basically.Kati Morton
  • Then I started on the anxiety meds which only made my anxiety 10 times worse.Kati Morton

Common word groups

  • social anxiety
  • anxiety and depression
  • anxiety disorder
  • anxiety attack
  • severe anxiety
  • generalized anxiety
  • constant anxiety
  • anxiety meds

Usage note

Very often the head of a noun compound: social, separation, job, death anxiety, and the creative coinage trickle-down anxiety. It is usually listed together with depression. A counter-narrative also appears in the corpus: anxiety is not an enemy, it is a signal.

overthinking

noun / verb (overthink)

common, 45.6 per million

Thinking about something for too long or too much, so that it causes worry instead of helping you decide. Commenters use it to describe a habit or a state they are stuck in.

From the comments

  • I overthink everything I’ve said ever & I relive conversations & get wound up, every single day.Kati Morton
  • I won’t give my energy to overthinking anymore.Thewizardliz
  • It's the overthinking that creates the tug-of-war.Jonathan Bricker (TEDxRainier)
  • I'm always overthinking and analysing everything.Kati Morton
  • And she will just keep telling me to be positive and stop overthinking.Kati Morton

Common word groups

  • stop overthinking
  • always overthink
  • overthink everything
  • overthinking about
  • overthinking loop
  • stop overthinking it

Usage note

Typically used with always or with an object like everything. The imperative stop overthinking is often quoted as unhelpful advice from others. The informal spelling abt (about) is common after it.

discipline

noun

common, 82.5 per million

The ability to control your own behaviour and keep doing what you planned, built up through regular practice. In this corpus it almost always means self-discipline, not rules imposed by others.

From the comments

  • Discipline is choosing between what you want now and what you want most.Jonathan Bricker (TEDxRainier)
  • Start with the little disciplines because those will lead to the big ones.GrindBuddy
  • Discipline isn't a sacrifice, it is an enrichment.Jonathan Bricker (TEDxRainier)
  • Please first have the discipline to do what you planned.Frederik Imbo (TEDx)
  • I have lacked self discipline for a very long time.Jonathan Bricker (TEDxRainier)

Common word groups

  • self-discipline
  • little discipline
  • lack of discipline
  • build discipline
  • practice and discipline
  • discipline and training
  • be disciplined

Usage note

Strongly metaphorical: discipline is a gateway, an engine, freedom, and a discipline muscle you train. In general web English (English Web 2021) the word is institutional instead: academic or strict discipline that is imposed or enforced.

mindset

noun

frequent, 131.3 per million

A person's usual way of thinking and the attitudes that come with it. Commenters treat it as something you can change, fix or carry into a situation.

Common word groups

  • change your mindset
  • victim mindset
  • positive mindset
  • right mindset
  • growth mindset

Usage note

Often pre-modified by a type: victim, positive, growth, or a quoted phrase like the "I have to be right" mindset. Close neighbours are attitude, mentality and way of thinking. Watch out: many hits in the GrindBuddy comments come from spam advertising a money-mindset book.

self-education

noun

rare, 10.5 per million

Learning things by yourself, outside school or formal training, usually by reading and practising. In the comments it is praised as a key to success.

Common word groups

  • self-education is key

  • become self-educated

Usage note

Rare and concentrated in one video (GrindBuddy, quoting Jim Rohn), so the examples come from a single source. The adjective self-educated is as frequent as the noun. Part of a productive family with self-discipline, self-worth and self-motivation.

empathy

noun

frequent, 297.7 per million

The ability to understand and share another person's feelings. In this corpus it is also turned inwards: giving yourself empathy means treating yourself with understanding.

Common word groups

  • give yourself empathy
  • lack empathy
  • no empathy
  • feel empathy
  • show empathy
  • cognitive empathy
  • emotional empathy

Usage note

Two distinct uses. Self-directed: give yourself empathy, close to self-compassion (from the TEDx talk on not taking things personally). Clinical: in the narcissism video it is discussed as something people lack, fake or have in cognitive and emotional forms. Most hits come from that one video.

you are not alone

phrase

common, 48.3 per million

Said to someone who is struggling, to tell them that other people feel the same and understand them.

Common word groups

  • know you're not alone
  • know that you are not alone

  • realize you are not alone

Usage note

A reply formula: it usually answers another comment that describes a problem, and often comes with a heart emoji or a second reassurance like you matter. Both you are and you're are common; capitals (NOT ALONE) add emphasis.

healing

noun / verb (heal)

very frequent, 377 per million

The slow process of recovering from emotional pain or trauma. Commenters describe it as a path or journey they are on.

From the comments

  • Good luck on your healing journey xKati Morton
  • It is a painful process to heal.Kati Morton
  • I am learning to begin my healing journey as well.❤Joe Dispenza
  • Defense mechanisms and working thru trauma and learning to laugh at our past is so healing.Jordan Peterson & Matt Rife
  • Healing looks so beautiful on you 🤍Thewizardliz

Common word groups

  • heal from
  • healing journey
  • healing process
  • heal myself
  • spiritual healing
  • so healing
  • laughter is healing

Usage note

Abstract and often spiritual, unlike the clinical anxiety. Heal from + a cause (trauma, abuse, a bad relationship) is the most frequent pattern; so healing works as an adjective meaning helpful for recovery.

mindfulness

noun / adjective (mindful)

common, 50.1 per million

Paying calm attention to what you are thinking and feeling in the present moment, without judging it. Often discussed as a meditation practice.

From the comments

  • Just like mindfulness meditation, you dont push the thought away you just notice it and allow without judgement.Jonathan Bricker (TEDxRainier)
  • So right now make a mindful decision to just take one minute at a time.Kati Morton
  • This truly has reinforced my confidence to practice being mindful.Frederik Imbo (TEDx)
  • Mindfulness, meditations usually is my antidote but too exhausting.Kati Morton
  • This talk can be summed up in one word - Mindfulness.Jonathan Bricker (TEDxRainier)

Common word groups

  • mindfulness meditation
  • mindfulness practice
  • practice mindfulness
  • be mindful
  • mindful of
  • mindful meditation

Usage note

Mostly comes up when commenters compare a talk's advice to mindfulness, sometimes critically (a repackaging of mindfulness). Be mindful of + noun means pay attention to.
